Eastern Algarve Private Tour: Faro, Tavira and the Ria Formosa

The quiet half of the Algarve: Faro’s walled old town, the lagoons of the Ria Formosa, the clifftop village of Cacela Velha, and Tavira on both banks of its river.

01 The day

From Algarve, door to door

Less coast road, more towns and water. The east is flatter and calmer than the west.

Start+8hFaro old townRia FormosaTaviraCacela Velha
  1. Start

    Your hotel

    Collected anywhere in the Algarve. The times here are from Vilamoura.

  2. +45m

    Faro old town

    Through the Arco da Vila into the walled town, to the cathedral square and the Chapel of Bones at Carmo.

    Chapel entrance not included
  3. +2h30

    The Ria Formosa

    A lagoon of channels, salt pans and barrier islands protected as a natural park. Boats go out to the islands from Faro and Olhão.

    Boat trip not included
  4. +4h30

    Tavira, and lunch

    Whitewashed houses on both sides of the Gilão, the old bridge between them, and the castle garden above. Lunch by the river.

  5. +6h30

    Cacela Velha

    A tiny village on a cliff above the lagoon, with a fort, a church and one of the best views in the Algarve.

  6. +8h

    Back to your hotel

    In the evening.

04 Questions

Before you ask

Does the price depend on the size of the group?

Yes. The tour is private, and the price depends on how many of you travel, starting from €240. Choose the number of people when you book.

Can we add a boat trip to the islands?

Yes. We can drop you at the jetty in Olhão or Faro and collect you when you are back.

Can we do the western cliffs instead?

Yes. Our Benagil, Lagos and Sagres day covers the west.

Are there more than 16 of us?

Two vehicles travelling together, on one booking. Message us and we will price it.

Can we cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before departure, at no cost. After that we cannot refund the day. If we cancel because conditions are unsafe, you are refunded in full.

05 Booking

How to book

Tell us the day and how many of you there are. We check it with the operator who runs that date and come back within one working day with the exact price for your group and a hold on the day. Nothing is charged until you say yes.

Ask about a date

Cancellation

Cancel up to 24 hours before your departure time and you pay nothing. Inside 24 hours the vehicle and the driver are already committed to your day, so we cannot refund it.

Weather is not a cancellation. These are private days: if a stop is genuinely unsafe or a road is closed, your driver changes the route and tells you why, and you still get a full day out. That is the thing a coach tour cannot do.

If we have to cancel — conditions that make the roads unsafe, a vehicle that fails its check — you are refunded in full, whenever it happens.
