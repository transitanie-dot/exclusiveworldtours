Private day tour in Sardinia

Costa Smeralda and Gallura Private Day Tour

The granite coast of north-east Sardinia, where the water is the colour the name promises: Porto Cervo, the beaches of the Costa Smeralda, Palau and the wind-carved rocks of Capo Testa.

The day

Bring a swimsuit: the day is built so you can stop at a beach and actually use it.

Start+8hPorto CervoBeachesSan PantaleoPalauCapo Testa
  1. Start

    Your hotel

    Collected in Olbia or anywhere on the Costa Smeralda.

  2. +45m

    Porto Cervo

    The village the Aga Khan built in the 1960s for the yachts: a marina, a piazza and a church on the rocks.

  3. +2h

    The beaches

    Capriccioli, or another of the coves on the Costa Smeralda — white sand, granite and clear water.

  4. +3h30

    San Pantaleo, and lunch

    A granite village in the hills behind the coast, with its square and its peaks.

  5. +5h

    Palau and the Bear Rock

    The port for the Maddalena islands, and the rock above it shaped like a bear.

  6. +6h

    Capo Testa

    The northern tip of Sardinia, with Corsica across the strait and the Valle della Luna carved by the wind.

  7. +8h

    Back to your hotel

    In the evening.

What the price covers

The price covers the vehicle and the driver.

  • Private vehicle and driver for the whole day
  • Fuel, tolls and parking
  • Itinerary changed on the day

Lunch, beach services and boat trips are not included.

Practical

La MaddalenaBoats to the Maddalena archipelago leave from Palau. We can build the day around one — ask us.
SeasonJuly and August are the busiest months on the coast. June and September are calmer and still warm.

Questions

Does the price depend on the size of the group?

Yes. The tour is private, and the price depends on how many of you travel, starting from €490. Choose the number of people when you book.

Do you collect from Olbia airport or the port?

Yes. Message us with your flight or ferry and we will price it.

Can we see more of the island?

Yes. Ask us about the Gallura hills, or about the south of the island from Cagliari.

Are there more than 16 of us?

Two vehicles travelling together, on one booking. Message us and we will price it.
