Apply to sell your tours

Six questions about your company and one about a tour you are proud of. A person reads it — not a form that scores you — and you get an answer either way.

Your company

This becomes your sign-in for the operator portal. Use one you will keep.
If your country issues one. We do not invent a requirement that does not exist where you are.
Because we price by vehicle, this is what decides your price tiers.
This is the part that decides the application. Write it as you would tell a guest, not as a brochure.

Got it

We read applications ourselves and answer every one, including the ones we turn down. Expect a reply within two working days.

If we go ahead, we link your email to your company and you sign in at the operator portal — no password, just a link we email you.

The terms, in three lines

Commission is 20% of the price the guest pays. You set the price; we do not change it and we do not discount your tour to win a sale.

Your content is read by a person before it goes on the site, and read again on every change.

Your calendar is not reviewed at all. You close a day, it is closed — immediately.

What we ask

That you run the tour yourself, hold whatever licence your country requires, price the whole vehicle rather than the seat, own your photographs, and keep the calendar honest.

What we are not pretending

We are new. We will not show you visitor numbers, because they would not impress you and a number invented to impress you is the first thing you would check.
