On the road 3 October 2026

The early start is the whole trick

Look at the departure times on these tours and a pattern appears. The ones that leave first are the ones that go where the coaches go.

Why the hour is in the data

Every tour on this site carries its departure time, and we show it on the card before you click. It is not a detail. On a route with a famous stop, the difference between leaving at seven and leaving at nine is the difference between a cliff edge and a car park.

A coach cannot leave at seven. It has to collect from four hotels first, and it has to be back for the evening run. A private vehicle collects from one door.

What you give up

An early start costs you the morning. Some days that is the wrong trade and the right answer is a later departure and a quieter route — which is a thing you can ask for, because the day is yours.

If the departure time on a page does not suit you, say so when you ask about the date. It is a starting point written by the operator who drives the route, not a rule.
